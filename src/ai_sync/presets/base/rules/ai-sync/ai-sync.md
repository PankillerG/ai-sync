Skills and rules for AI agents are managed by ai-sync. When the user asks to create, change, or delete a skill or a rule, edit its source in `~/.config/ai-sync/` or `<project>/.ai-sync/` and run `ai-sync deploy`. Never write skills or rules directly into provider directories such as `~/.claude/skills/`, `~/.claude/rules/`, `~/.agents/skills/`, `~/.config/opencode/` or `AGENTS.md`. Use the `ai-sync` skill for the details.
